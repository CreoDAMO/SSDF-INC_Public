05 — Decision rule

Four questions before a bid.

  1. 01Can SSDF legitimately contract to deliver the requirement?
  2. 02Can SSDF obtain the required people, equipment, facilities, licenses, certifications, and other resources?
  3. 03Can SSDF perform directly or through employees, independent contractors, qualified subcontractors, vendors, or teaming partners where permitted?
  4. 04What solicitation-specific limitations prevent or condition that structure?

Only then: price, contract, execute. Capability is not authorization. Contract-specific rules control.

06 — Disaster response

Registry: yes. Geography: all United States.

The Disaster Response Registry is an additional federal distribution channel — not a claim that SSDF maintains every emergency-response resource in every location. The operating model is the same: requirement → scope → qualification → build/buy/partner → labor and equipment → SSDF control layer → execution → evidence → delivery → invoice.

A listed NAICS or PSC code is a contracting capability, not a claim that every labor or equipment resource is currently on payroll.
